# Changelog

## Unreleased

First public release.

- A local page (`kroger-pp-cli serve`) for filling your Kroger cart from your
  staples, search, recipes and past trips, with the cart beside every tab and
  changes sent together by **Update Kroger cart**.
- Staples built from your own purchase history, with prices, pictures and
  stock, and a saved "usual" that one click brings the cart up to.
- Out-of-stock items are marked and can't be added, from the page, the server
  or `scripts/kroger-weekly-cart`.
- Sign-in straight from Chrome (`auth from-chrome`) and one-paste store setup
  (`auth set-store`), plus `SETUP.md` so an AI agent can do the whole setup.
